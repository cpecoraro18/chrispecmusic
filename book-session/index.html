How it works

  1. Send your track

    Send a rough mix or demo, along with the tempo and any notes on style, tone, or feel. References or a rough guide bass line help, but they are not required.

  2. We agree on the details

    I'll come back with a plan and a quote. If you're not sure what you want the bass to do, I'm happy to suggest a few directions.

  3. I record your bass

    I cut two or three takes with different approaches so you have options, recorded and edited in my studio.

  4. You request changes

    Revisions are included, so I'll adjust the part, tone, or feel until it fits the song.

  5. You get the files

    Mix-ready stems land in your inbox with a payment link. Once the project is paid, the recordings are yours to use however you like.
