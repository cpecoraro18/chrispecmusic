How it works

  1. Send your track

    Send a rough mix or demo with the tempo and any notes on style, tone, or feel. References or a guide bass line help but aren't required.

  2. We agree on the details

    I'll reply with a plan and a quote. Not sure what the bass should do? I'll suggest a few directions.

  3. I record your bass

    I record two or three takes, each with a different approach, so you have options.

  4. You request changes

    Two rounds of revisions are included to adjust the part, tone, or feel.

  5. You get the files

    Mix-ready stems arrive with a payment link. Once paid, the recordings are yours to use however you like.
